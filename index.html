Hand control

Tap Hand. Gestures act immediately when recognized:
Open palm (all fingers + thumb) → Relay 1 on · Fist (including thumb closed) → Relay 1 off
Index + thumb (L-sign) → Relay 2 on · Peace sign → Relay 2 off
Each gesture changes only its selected relay.

Safety timeout is on. Relays switch off if this page loses its connection to the ESP32.
